Invitation Studio

Version 1.2.0 • Premium Web-First Keepsakes

Invitation Studio is an elegant, premium, and fully-customizable digital invitation platform. We enable couples, hosts, and event managers to build exquisite, interactive mobile and web-first wedding invitations.

Leveraging powerful drag-and-drop mechanics, rich custom typography, dynamic background music controls, instant RSVP guest list management, and elegant premium templates, Invitation Studio turns a simple wedding announcement into an unforgettable digital keepsake.

Crafted with precision, our mission is to blend fine art and state-of-the-art web technology, providing couples and event hosts with the ultimate medium to welcome their beloved guests with sophistication.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

1. Introduction

At Invitation Studio, your privacy is of paramount importance. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, secure, and process your personal information when you use our services.

2. Data We Collect

We collect your Google Account profile details (such as your full name, email address, and profile photo) during the Google Sign-In authentication process. While designing invitations, we process event metadata, RSVP responses, guest attendance, and customized text/image inputs.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your information is strictly used to authenticate your session, sync your designs with cloud storage, display your public invitation pages, and allow you to view, filter, and export guest RSVP responses.

4. Data Retention & Security

We securely store your invitation drafts, templates, and published details on Google Firebase Cloud Firestore. We implement strict server-side security rules to ensure that unauthorized third parties cannot edit or read your private draft configurations.

5. Your Rights

You have full control over your data. You can delete your invitations, clear guest lists, or modify any saved draft permanently at any time directly through your dashboard.
